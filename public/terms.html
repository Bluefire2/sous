What Sous is

Sous is a personal recipe book with an AI cooking assistant. It is free and invitation-only: access is granted by request and approval, or by redeeming a single-use invite link sent by someone who already has access. The owner can remove access at any time. It comes with no guarantee of availability. Your recipe library lives in your account and loads when you are signed in. Cached translations of your recipes are stored in Firestore under your account and are deleted when their recipe is deleted. Translations of recipes shared with you are not stored; they are translated again each session. You can share a named collection with another admitted member by email, or with a link that admitted members can use to join until you revoke it or it expires. A viewer can see those recipes and photos. An editor can also change the recipes' details (such as title, ingredients, steps, notes, servings, cooking times, and tags), but not their photos, and cannot delete them or change who has access. Sharing does not create a household account. You can also make a named collection public: anyone with its link can then read its recipes and photos without an account, until you turn the link off. Visitors without an account cannot use the cooking assistant or translation. Only make public what you have the right to share.

You can connect an AI app, such as Claude, to your library. After you allow it on Sous's consent page, it can search and read your own recipes and, if you allowed editing, add and edit them and move them between your collections, including into ones you share with other members (it never adds recipes to a collection with a public link); it cannot see recipes shared with you, photos, or your cook log, and cannot delete anything. The app's provider receives the recipe text the app reads or writes, under that provider's terms. What a connected app does with your recipes, and any edit it saves, is that app's doing, made with the access you gave it. Disconnect it in Settings → Connected apps; signing out does not disconnect it. Each request it makes and each step of connecting it is logged for 30 days without recipe text, as the Privacy policy's Server logs section describes.

Accuracy

On a recipe, that recipe's text is sent to Google's Gemini API at request time to answer your questions. When you use the library assistant (Ask, at /assistant), many recipes from your library — titles, tags, and the full text of recipes the assistant looks up — not only the recipe you are viewing, are sent to Gemini at request time to produce the reply.

The assistant, dictation, recipe translation, and the recipe importer are AI and can be wrong — including about cooking times, temperatures, substitutions, quantities and food safety, and about what you said into the microphone. Recipe text is sent to Gemini, or the configured translation provider, for translation, including at import. Recipes read from photos, especially handwriting, can have wrong or missing amounts; check them in the preview before saving. Those photos are sent to Google's Gemini API to extract the recipe and are not stored. Each import is logged for 30 days with your account id and its outcome, plus the page address (without its query string) when it came from a link or the extension, so failed imports can be fixed; the Privacy policy's Server logs section describes what Sous's logs hold. If an import goes wrong you can choose to send an import report; it holds the link or the text you pasted, the recipe the import extracted, and any note you add, and it is deleted after about 180 days. Photos are never included. Use your own judgement and check anything that matters.

A recipe Sous writes from an idea you type is made up by the model, not copied from a tested source: check amounts, times, temperatures, and food safety before you cook it. If you turn on Search the web, Gemini may run Google searches for your idea, and the pages it used are shown with the result together with Google's search suggestions. An import report for such a recipe holds the idea you typed in place of pasted text.

If you fill in Kitchen profile in Settings, it is sent to Gemini with your questions on a recipe, your library assistant messages, and recipes written from an idea, so the answers can take your allergies, diet, and equipment into account. That is a request to the model, not a guarantee: it can still miss an allergen, including one hidden in a prepared ingredient. Always check a recipe for allergens yourself.

Acceptable use

Sous is for your own recipes and normal personal use. Do not try to break or probe the service, and do not use it to generate abuse. AI features have a daily limit per account, far above normal use; once it is reached they stop working until midnight UTC.

Ending it

You can stop at any time: sign out, clear this site's browser data on a device, revoke Sous's access from your Google account permissions page, or disconnect connected apps in Settings. The UI language preference is kept in this browser's local storage under cook.locale. To remove all data stored for your account, see the Privacy policy. The service may also change or stop at any time.
